Let's practice "Is this a valid BST or not?" using MCQs, starting with easy questions and progressing to tricky LeetCode-style examples.

Remember these two important rules:

1. Every value in the left subtree must be smaller than the current node.
2. Every value in the right subtree must be larger than the current node.


1. Is this a valid BST?
    8
   / \
  4   12
 / \  / \
2  6 10 14

Ans => Correct!
Every left-subtree value is smaller than its ancestor, and every right-subtree value is larger.


2. Is this a valid BST?

    8
   / \
  10  12

Ans => Correct answer: No
10 is the left child of 8, but 10 > 8. The left child must be smaller.


3. Is this a valid BST?
    8
   / \
  4   6

Correct answer: No
6 is in the right subtree of 8, but 6 < 8. Being greater than its parent 4 is not enough.


4. Is this a valid BST?
     10
    /  \
   5    15
       /
      12

Correct = Yes 
5 < 10; 12 is greater than 10 but smaller than 15; and 15 > 10. All ordering rules hold.


5. Is this a valid BST?
     10
    /  \
   5    15
       /
       8
Correct answer: No
8 is in the right subtree of 10, so it must be greater than 10. But 8 < 10.


6. Is this a valid BST?
     20
    /  \
   10   30
     \
      25
Correct answer: No
25 is in the left subtree of 20, so it must be less than 20. Although 25 > 10, it violates the ancestor rule.


7. Is this a valid BST?
    5
   / \
  3   7
 /     \
2       9

Ans = Yes 
2 < 3 < 5 and 9 > 7 > 5. All values obey the ancestor constraints.


8. Is this a valid BST under strict ordering (no duplicates)?
    5
   / \
  3   7
   \
    5

Ans = No 

Correct!
The duplicate 5 violates strict BST ordering. Standard validation problems generally disallow duplicate values.

10. Is this a valid BST?
     10
    /  \
   5    15
    \
     12
Ans => No 
12 is in the left subtree of 10, so it must be smaller than 10. But 12 > 10.

The most important trick for LeetCode
Consider this tree:

      10
     /  \
    5    15
     \
      12

At first glance, it might look valid because 12 > 5.
But it is not a valid BST because 12 is in the left subtree of 10, and every value in that subtree must be smaller than 10.
This is one of the most common mistakes in LeetCode's 98. Validate Binary Search Tree.
